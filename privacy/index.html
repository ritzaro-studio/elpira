Privacy Policy

Your privacy, handled simply.

Effective date: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Elpira handles information when you use the app.

Overview

Elpira is designed as a simple wellbeing app for daily affirmations, journaling, breathing exercises, and gentle reminders. The app does not require you to create an account.

Information stored on your device

Elpira may store app preferences on your device, such as selected reminder times, favorite affirmations, premium access status, and journal entries.

Journal entries are intended to remain on your device. Depending on your device settings, operating system backups may include app data.

Reminders and notifications

Elpira uses local notifications to deliver reminders that you choose inside the app. These reminders are scheduled on your device.

Purchases and subscriptions

If you choose to purchase Elpira Premium, payments and subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device. Elpira does not receive or store your payment card details.

Third-party services

Elpira may rely on Apple App Store and Google Play services for app distribution, purchases, subscriptions, and restore purchase functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

No sale of personal data

We do not sell your personal data.

Children

Elpira is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health and wellbeing disclaimer

Elpira provides general wellbeing content only. It is not a medical, mental health, therapy, or crisis support service. If you need medical or mental health support, please contact a qualified professional or emergency services in your area.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
